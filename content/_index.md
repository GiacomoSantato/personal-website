# Giacomo Santato

I’m a last-year PhD student at [CISPA](https://cispa.de), working in Antoine Joux’s research group.

My main focus is on Fully Homomorphic Encryption (FHE), but my work often extends into related areas like Multi-Party Computation (MPC) and Zero-Knowledge proofs (ZK).

Outside of research, I’m an active Capture The Flag (CTF) player with the [about:blankets](https://ctftime.org/team/208552) team, where I spend much of my free time tackling crypto challenges with friends.
