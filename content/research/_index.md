[J. Huth](https://cispa.de/en/people/c01jahu), [A. Joux](https://cispa.de/en/people/c01anjo), **G. Santato**

### **Dimensional eROSion: Improving the ROS Attack with Decomposition in Higher Bases**  
*Cryptology ePrint Archive, 2025*  
[A. Joux](https://cispa.de/en/people/c01anjo), [J. Loss](https://www.julianloss.com/), **G. Santato**  

We extend the polynomial ROS attack to work in lower dimensions using higher-base decompositions and some LLL magic.

### **On Circuit Private, Multikey and Threshold Approximate Homomorphic Encryption**  
*IACR Communications in Cryptology, vol 2, issue 1*  
[K. Kluczniak](https://www.linkedin.com/in/kamil-kluczniak-742b39ba/), **G. Santato**  

We analyze how to achieve circuit privacy in approximate multiparty FHE by proving that strong noise is essential, and encrypting the function is not adding security.    
🔗 [Published Version](https://cic.iacr.org/p/2/1/9) · [FHE.org talk](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9cX1TK4RYfY)
